# BizLink User Guide

> Everything a brand or entrepreneur needs to use BizLink — from first sign-in to closing a deal.

## Getting started

### Create your account

- Register with email + password, or continue with **Google** / **Facebook**.
- Brand-new social accounts pick an account type once: **Entrepreneur** (discover and inquire) or **Brand** (list opportunities). Email registration asks the same question up front.
- Demo accounts (password `password123`): `demo@bizlink.ph` (entrepreneur) · `brand@bizlink.ph` (brand) · `admin@bizlink.ph` (admin console).

### Your home

| You are… | You land on | You can… |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Entrepreneur | Feed | Browse, like, save, comment, inquire, post |
| Brand | Feed | Everything above, plus your listings get a gold **BRAND** pill |
| Admin | Admin console | Full platform controls (see Admin Guide) |

## Feed, Reels, Stories

- **Feed** (`/feed`) — opportunity cards with type badges (Franchise / Wholesale / Resell), capital and ROI chips. Filter chips narrow by type; Following surfaces followed brands first.
- **Comments** — tap the comment pill on any card to open the post overlay with the full thread: reply, react, edit (15 minutes), report abuse.
- **Reels** (`/reels`) — vertical pitch videos. They autoplay muted in view; tap to unmute, tap the play overlay to pause.
- **Daily Opportunities** — the story rail on top of the feed. Anyone's live stories appear here for 24 hours. Tap to watch, hold to pause.
- **Post page** (`/post/:slug`) — the full listing: Inquire button, like / save / comment counts, and the complete thread.

## Posting an opportunity (both roles)

1. Tap **+ Post** (navbar) or open `/create`.
2. Pick a type (Franchise / Wholesale / Resell), headline, capital, ROI %, description.
3. Add a photo (≤20MB) or video (≤100MB) — video posts also appear as reels with a smart thumbnail.
4. Publish — your post is live instantly and appears in your profile.

## Messenger

- **Inquire** on any post, reel, or story opens a private thread with that item pinned as a quote card.
- Send text, photos, videos, documents (≤25MB), polls, and schedule **Google Meet** calls inside the thread (connect Google once from Edit Profile).
- Meetings, polls, and shared insights render as rich cards. New messages arrive live; a reconnecting chip appears if the connection drops.
- Misbehaving counterpart? Use **Report** on a message, the conversation, or their profile — every report is reviewed by an administrator.

## Notifications, Saved, Profile

- The **bell** bumps live for replies, reactions, inquiries, and moderation decisions. `/notifications` keeps the full history.
- **Saved** is your shortlist, synced to your account.
- **Profile** shows your posts and reels grids; **Preferences** tunes feed ranking to your categories and budget.

## Staying safe

- Never send money before verifying a business independently — BizLink introduces parties but is not part of any deal.
- Only the gold **BRAND** + green **✓ Verified** combination means an administrator reviewed the business. Either badge alone means less — read both.
- See [Acceptable Use](/acceptable-use), [Terms](/terms), and [Privacy](/privacy). Questions: `support@bizlink.ph`.
